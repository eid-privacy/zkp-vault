# A Short-List of Pairing-Friendly Curves Resistant to the Special TNFS Algorithm at the 192-bit Security Level (Aranha, Fotiadis, Guillevic 2024)

## Summary
Re-evaluates pairing-friendly curve families against the special tower number field sieve attack and recommends curves for 192-bit security, with BLS24-509 as the most efficient choice (IACR Communications in Cryptology 2024). [[BL26-SoK-AnonCreds]] cites it in its discussion of pairing curves for BBS credentials: BLS12-381 is the de-facto 128-bit choice, but it still lacks a formal standard.
