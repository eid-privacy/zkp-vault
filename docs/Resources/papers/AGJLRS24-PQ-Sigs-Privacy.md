# Practical Post-Quantum Signatures for Privacy (Argo, Güneysu, Jeudy, Land, Roux-Langlois, Sanders 2024)

## Summary
Proposes a lattice-based signature scheme with efficient protocols (signing committed messages and proving knowledge of a signature in ZK), the post-quantum analogue of the CL/PS/BBS family, and uses it to build anonymous credentials and related privacy primitives with practical sizes and an optimized implementation. Relevant to e-ID as a candidate for post-quantum unlinkable credentials.
