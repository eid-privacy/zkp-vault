# Improved Algebraic MACs and Practical Keyed-Verification Anonymous Credentials (Barki, Brunet, Desmoulins, Traoré 2016)

## Summary
Presents a new algebraic MAC derived from BBS+ and builds keyed-verification anonymous credentials (where issuer and verifier share a key) with presentation proofs that are cheaper than prior KVAC schemes and need no pairings (SAC 2016). Also proves the security of a more efficient BBS+ variant, which is why it is cited by the IRTF BBS draft. The same Orange group later designed BBS#.
