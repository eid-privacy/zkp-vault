# SoK: Technical Implementation and Human Impact of Internet Privacy Regulations (Birrell et al. 2024)

## Summary
Systematizes research on how internet privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA and others) are technically implemented and how they affect people in practice, e.g. consent banners, data-subject access requests and opt-outs. Finds frequent gaps between legal intent, deployed mechanisms and user outcomes. Relevant to e-ID because legal requirements such as data minimisation and unlinkability (eIDAS 2.0, Swiss e-ID law) are what motivate ZKP-based credentials. Published at IEEE S&P 2024.
