# A Signature Scheme with Efficient Protocols (Camenisch, Lysyanskaya 2002)

## Summary
Presents the CL signature scheme (strong RSA assumption) together with efficient protocols to obtain a signature on committed values and to prove knowledge of a signature on committed messages. These two protocols are exactly what anonymous credentials need, and CL signatures became the basis of IBM Idemix and Hyperledger AnonCreds. (SCN 2002.)
