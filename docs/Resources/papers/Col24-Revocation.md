# Scalable and Privacy-Preserving Revocation of Verifiable Credentials (Colombo 2024)

## Summary
EPFL Master's thesis (September 2024, with the Cyber-Defence Campus) on revocation for the Swiss e-ID. Designs and implements a pairing-based accumulator scheme with zero-knowledge membership proofs compatible with BBS credentials, focusing on efficient witness updates at national scale; the Rust proof-of-concept is published alongside the thesis.
