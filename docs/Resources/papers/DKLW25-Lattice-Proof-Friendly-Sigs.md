# Lattice-Based Proof-Friendly Signatures from Vanishing Short Integer Solutions (Dubois, Klooß, Lai, Woo 2025)

## Summary
Proposes lattice-based signatures designed to admit efficient zero-knowledge proofs of knowledge of a signature, based on a new "vanishing short integer solutions" assumption (PKC 2025). They are candidates for post-quantum anonymous credentials in the spirit of BBS or PS. [[BL26-SoK-AnonCreds]] lists it among the quantum-safe multi-message signatures with efficient proofs, noting that the new assumptions need more study.
