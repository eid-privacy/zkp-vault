# SoK: Anonymous Credentials (Kakvi, Martin, Putman, Quaglia 2023)

## Summary
General systematization of anonymous credential schemes presented at SSR 2023: it organizes the literature by underlying signature technique and by the properties and functionalities that anonymous credentials offer. [[BL26-SoK-AnonCreds]] positions itself as the wallet-centric complement to this broader SoK.
