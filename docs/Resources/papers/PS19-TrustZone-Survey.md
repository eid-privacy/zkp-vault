# Demystifying Arm TrustZone: A Comprehensive Survey (Pinto, Santos 2019)

## Summary
Comprehensive survey (ACM Computing Surveys) of Arm TrustZone, the hardware isolation technology behind the trusted execution environments in most smartphones, covering its architecture, TEE systems and hardware-assisted virtualization from academia and industry, plus known attacks. Relevant to e-ID holder binding, where wallet keys live in TrustZone-based keystores and trusted hardware assumptions underpin device binding.
