# Blind BBS Signatures — IRTF CFRG draft

## Summary
Extension of the CFRG BBS draft that lets a holder commit to messages (e.g. a holder secret or attributes hidden from the issuer) and obtain a BBS signature over them without revealing them. This enables (partially) blind issuance and binding credentials to a holder secret. Cited in [[BL26-SoK-AnonCreds]] as one of the IETF specifications extending the BBS core scheme.
