# Ordinance on the E-ID Act (E-ID-Verordnung, VEID) — consultation draft

## Summary
Federal Council consultation (opened 20 June 2025) on the implementing ordinance of the Swiss E-ID Act. The draft regulates eligibility and issuance of the E-ID, how verifiers use the trust infrastructure and issue their own credentials, limits on data that verifiers may request and store, and a complaint procedure that can flag over-asking verifiers in the trust registry. The linked page provides the draft ordinance and explanatory report.
