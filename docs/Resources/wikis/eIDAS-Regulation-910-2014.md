# Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS)

## Summary
EU regulation on electronic identification and trust services for electronic transactions in the internal market (eIDAS), establishing mutual recognition of notified national eID schemes and the legal framework for electronic signatures, seals and trust service providers. Amended in 2024 by Regulation (EU) 2024/1183, which introduced the European Digital Identity Wallet.
